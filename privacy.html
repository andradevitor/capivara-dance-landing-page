🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

📋 Overview

Capivara Dance is a free arcade game. We built it to be simple and fun — and that means zero data collection, zero tracking, and zero account creation. This policy explains exactly what we do (and don't) do with your information.

📵 No Data Collected

Capivara Dance does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. This includes:

  • No name, email, or account details
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No cookies or tracking technologies

📱 Local Storage Only

Your best score is saved locally on your device using the device's own storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any third party.

🚫 No Ads & No Purchases

The game contains no advertisements, no in-app purchases, and no third-party SDKs. There are no analytics frameworks embedded in the app.

👶 Children's Privacy

Because we collect no data whatsoever, Capivara Dance is safe for players of all ages, including children under 13, and is compliant with COPPA and similar regulations.

✏️ Changes to This Policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this page with a new date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
